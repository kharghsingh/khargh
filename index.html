Good to know

Before we begin.

A few useful details before you arrange an initial discussion.

Do you take both residential and commercial projects?

Elite Space is positioned for residential interiors and commercial spaces such as shops, showrooms, cafés and offices. The final scope depends on the project requirements and execution capacity.

Can I discuss a renovation only?

Yes. A first discussion can focus on renovation or selected work such as civil coordination, finishing, furniture, lighting or material planning. The exact services should be confirmed for each site.

How is a project quotation prepared?

After understanding the scope and, where practical, measuring the site, a quotation can list included work, material assumptions, exclusions, timeline and payment milestones.

Do you provide 2D or 3D designs?

Design and visualisation options can be discussed according to the space and the needs of the project. Any design deliverables and fees should be agreed before work starts.
